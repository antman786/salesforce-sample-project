## Problem
Sales representatives currently struggle with fragmented legacy spreadsheets, which makes managing daily work difficult.

## Hypothesized value
If this is addressed, the team will benefit from a unified platform that centralizes account, contact, and opportunity data.

## Evidence
- "The objective is to replace fragmented legacy spreadsheets and an outdated ticketing system with a unified Customer Relationship Management (CRM) platform."
- "our reps just need one place to track quotes and hand off new accounts to support without emailing spreadsheets around."
- "We have been living out of spreadsheets and an old Zendesk setup for way too long, so my main priority here is getting clean visibility into our freight pipeline"
