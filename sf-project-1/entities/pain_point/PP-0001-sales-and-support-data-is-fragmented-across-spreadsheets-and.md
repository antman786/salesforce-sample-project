## Description
The sales and support teams rely on fragmented legacy spreadsheets and an outdated Zendesk setup to manage their daily work. This makes it difficult to maintain visibility into the freight pipeline and leads to operational inefficiencies.

## Current workaround
The teams are living out of spreadsheets and emailing them around to track quotes and hand off new accounts.

## Evidence
- "We have been living out of spreadsheets and an old Zendesk setup for way too long, so my main priority here is getting clean visibility into our freight pipeline"
- "our reps just need one place to track quotes and hand off new accounts to support without emailing spreadsheets around."
